- A ritual described on *The Quitenssential Secret Divinations of the Qin and Han Dynasty*
- Made [[Klein Moretti (Zhou Mingrui)]] transmigrate

### Requirements (ch. 2)
- Four portions of a regional food

### How to do (ch. 2)
1. Place the four portions of food in the four corners of a room
2. Standing in the middle of the room, take four steps in a counterclockwise direction to make a square
3. To do the first step, chant "*The Immortal Lord of Heaven and Earth for Blessings*"
4. To do the second step, chant "*The Sky Lord of Heaven and Earth for Blessings*"
5. To do the third step, chant "*The Exalted Thearch of Heaven and Earth for Blessings*"
6. To do the fourth and final step, chant "*The Celestial* Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings"
7. Close your eyes and wait five minutes in the spot to complete the ritual
